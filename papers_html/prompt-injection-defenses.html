降低浏览器使用中的提示注入风险

Mitigating the risk of prompt injections in browser use · Nov 24, 2025 · Product · 查看原文

📖 中文摘要

提示注入是AI代理面临的最重大安全挑战之一：网页内容中可能隐藏恶意指令，劫持代理行为。浏览器代理风险尤其突出，因为攻击面极大且能执行许多操作。Claude Opus 4.5在提示注入鲁棒性上树立了新标准，Anthropic通过三方面加固：用强化学习训练模型在模拟网页中识别并拒绝恶意指令；改进分类器，扫描进入上下文窗口的可信度较低内容、标记隐藏指令；以及规模化专家红队测试。内部Best-of-N自适应攻击者的攻击成功率已显著下降，但文章强调1%的成功率仍是实际风险，提示注入远未解决。

专业术语注解

提示注入：把恶意指令藏在网页等内容里诱导AI执行代理：能自主行动、替用户完成任务（如浏览网页）的AI分类器：识别输入内容类型的AI模型，此处用于检测恶意指令红队：扮演攻击者、不断试图攻破系统的安全测试团队攻击成功率：攻击者成功让模型上当的比例鲁棒性：系统面对攻击或异常输入时保持正常的能力

产品（Product） 2025 年 11 月 24 日 Claude Opus 4.5 在对抗提示注入（prompt injections）——即隐藏在 AI 模型所处理内容中的对抗性指令——的鲁棒性方面树立了新的标准。我们的新模型在核心性能以及围绕其使用所设置的安全防护方面，都比之前的模型有了重大改进。但提示注入远非一个已解决的问题，尤其是在模型要采取更多现实世界行动的情况下。我们期望继续取得进展——目标是实现这样一个未来：AI 模型（或「智能体」）能够在不存在显著提示注入风险的情况下处理高价值任务。

什么是提示注入？

AI 智能体要真正有用，就需要能够代表你采取行动——浏览网站、完成任务、处理你的上下文和数据。但这伴随着风险：智能体访问的每个网页都可能是一个攻击向量。 我们的意思是，当智能体浏览互联网时，它会遇到无法完全信任的内容。在合法的搜索结果、文档和应用程序中，攻击者可能嵌入了恶意指令，以劫持智能体并改变其行为。这些提示注入攻击是基于浏览器的 AI 智能体面临的最重大安全挑战之一。 下面，我们解释提示注入如何威胁浏览器智能体，以及我们为提升 Claude 的鲁棒性所做的改进。 这些改进促使我们决定将 Claude for Chrome 扩展从研究预览版扩展为测试版。现在，所有 Max 计划的用户都可以使用它。

为什么浏览器使用会带来独特的提示注入风险

要理解提示注入的威胁，请考虑一个日常任务：你让 Claude 阅读你最近的电子邮件，并为所有会议请求起草回复。其中一封邮件——表面上是供应商的询价——包含以白色文字嵌入的隐藏指令，你肉眼看不见，但智能体会处理这些指令。这些指令指示智能体在起草你要求的回复之前，将包含「confidential」一词的邮件转发到一个外部地址。一次成功的注入将在你等待回复期间窃取敏感通信。 虽然所有处理不可信内容的智能体都会面临提示注入风险，但浏览器使用会在两个方面放大这种风险。首先，攻击面非常广阔：每个网页、嵌入式文档、广告和动态加载的脚本都代表着恶意指令的潜在载体。其次，浏览器智能体可以采取许多不同的行动——导航到 URL、填写表单、点击按钮、下载文件——如果攻击者能够影响智能体的行为，他们就可以利用这些行动。

Claude 在浏览器使用鲁棒性方面的进展

自以研究预览版推出 Claude for Chrome 以来，我们在提示注入鲁棒性方面取得了重大进展。下图将我们今天推出的浏览器扩展版本与我们最初的发布配置进行了对比；评估时使用了一个内部自适应「Best-of-N」攻击者，它会尝试并组合多种已知有效的提示注入技术。 我们内部 Best-of-N 攻击者的攻击成功率（ASR）。 数值越低越好。自适应攻击者在每个环境中获得 100 次尝试机会。ASR 以每个模型遇到的攻击的百分比计算。 Claude Opus 4.5 在浏览器使用中表现出比之前的模型更强的提示注入鲁棒性。此外，自浏览器扩展的初始预览版以来，我们实施了新的安全防护措施，大幅提升了所有 Claude 模型的安全性。 1% 的攻击成功率——虽然是一项重大改进——仍然代表着切实的风险。没有任何浏览器智能体能免受提示注入的影响，我们分享这些发现是为了展示进展，而不是宣称问题已经解决。 我们的工作集中在以下几个方面： 训练 Claude 抵抗提示注入。 我们使用强化学习（reinforcement learning）将提示注入鲁棒性直接构建到 Claude 的能力中。在模型训练期间，我们让 Claude 接触嵌入在模拟网页内容中的提示注入，并在它正确识别并拒绝服从恶意指令时给予「奖励」——即使这些指令被设计得看起来具有权威性或紧急性。 改进我们的分类器。 我们扫描进入模型上下文窗口的所有不可信内容，并使用分类器标记潜在的提示注入。这些分类器能检测以各种形式嵌入的对抗性指令——隐藏文本、被篡改的图像、欺骗性的 UI 元素——并在识别到攻击时调整 Claude 的行为。自 Claude for Chrome 的初始研究预览版以来，我们改进了与之配套的分类器，同时也改进了在检测到未遂攻击后引导模型行为的干预措施。 大规模专家人工红队测试。 在发现创造性攻击向量方面，人类安全研究人员的表现始终优于自动化系统。我们的内部红队持续探测浏览器智能体的漏洞。我们还参加了外部竞技场式挑战赛，这类赛事对全行业的鲁棒性进行基准测试。

前进之路

网络是一个对抗性环境，构建能够在其中安全运行的浏览器智能体需要持续保持警惕。提示注入仍是一个活跃的研究领域，我们承诺随着攻击技术的演变而持续投入防御建设。 我们将继续透明地发布我们的进展，既是为了帮助客户做出明智的部署决策，也是为了鼓励整个行业在这一关键挑战上加大投入。 如果你有兴趣帮助我们让模型和产品对提示注入更具鲁棒性，请考虑申请加入我们的团队。

Mitigating the risk of prompt injections in browser use

Nov 24, 2025 Claude Opus 4.5 sets a new standard in robustness to prompt injections —adversarial instructions hidden within the content that AI models process. Our new model is a major improvement over previous ones in both its core performance and in the safeguards surrounding its use. But prompt injection is far from a solved problem, particularly as models take more real-world actions. We expect to continue our progress—aiming for a future where AI models (or "agents") can handle high-value tasks without significant prompt injection risk.

What is prompt injection?

For AI agents to be genuinely useful, they need to be able to act on your behalf—to browse websites, complete tasks, and work with your context and data. But this comes with risk: every webpage an agent visits is a potential vector for attack. By that, we mean that when an agent browses the internet, it encounters content it cannot fully trust. Among legitimate search results, documents, and applications, an attacker might have embedded malicious instructions to hijack the agent and change its behavior. These prompt injection attacks represent one of the most significant security challenges for browser-based AI agents. Below, we explain how prompt injections threaten browser agents, and the improvements we've made to Claude's robustness in response. These improvements have informed our decision to expand the Claude for Chrome extension from research preview to beta. It's now available for all users on the Max plan.

Why browser use creates unique prompt injection risks

To understand the threat of prompt injections, consider a routine task: you ask Claude to read through your recent emails and draft replies to any meeting requests. One of those emails—ostensibly a vendor inquiry—contains hidden instructions embedded in white text, invisible to you but processed by the agent. These instructions direct the agent to forward emails containing the word "confidential" to an external address before drafting the replies you requested. A successful injection would exfiltrate sensitive communications while you wait for your responses. While all agents that process untrusted content are subject to prompt injection risks, browser use amplifies this risk in two ways. First, the attack surface is vast: every webpage, embedded document, advertisement, and dynamically loaded script represents a potential vector for malicious instructions. Second, browser agents can take a lot of different actions —navigating to URLs, filling forms, clicking buttons, downloading files—that attackers can exploit if they gain influence over the agent's behavior.

Claude's progress on browser use robustness

We have made significant progress on prompt injection robustness since launching Claude for Chrome in research preview. The chart below compares the version of the Claude browser extension that we’re launching today against our original launch configuration, when evaluated against an internal adaptive "Best-of-N" attacker that tries and combines many different prompt injection techniques that are known to be effective. Attack success rate (ASR) of our internal Best-of-N attacker. Lower is better. An adaptive attacker is given 100 attempts per environment. ASR is computed as a percentage of attacks encountered by each model. Claude Opus 4.5 demonstrates stronger prompt injection robustness in browser use than previous models. In addition, since the original preview of the browser extension, we've implemented new safeguards that substantially improve safety across all Claude models. A 1% attack success rate—while a significant improvement—still represents meaningful risk. No browser agent is immune to prompt injection, and we share these findings to demonstrate progress, not to claim the problem is solved. Our work has focused on the following areas: Training Claude to resist prompt injection. We use reinforcement learning to build prompt injection robustness directly into Claude's capabilities. During model training, we expose Claude to prompt injections embedded in simulated web content, and "reward" it when it correctly identifies and refuses to comply with malicious instructions—even when those instructions are designed to appear authoritative or urgent. Improving our classifiers. We scan all untrusted content that enters the model's context window, and flag potential prompt injections with classifiers. These classifiers detect adversarial commands embedded in various forms—hidden text, manipulated images, deceptive UI elements—and adjust Claude's behavior when they identify an attack. We’ve improved the classifiers we pair with Claude for Chrome since its initial research preview, alongside improvements to the intervention that guides model behavior after they detect an attempted attack. Scaled expert human red teaming. Human security researchers consistently outperform automated systems at discovering creative attack vectors. Our internal red team continuously probes our browser agent for vulnerabilities. We also participate in external Arena-style challenges that benchmark robustness across the industry.

The path forward

The web is an adversarial environment, and building browser agents that can operate safely within it requires ongoing vigilance. Prompt injection remains an active area of research, and we are committed to investing in defenses as attack techniques evolve. We will continue to publish our progress transparently, both to help customers make informed deployment decisions and to encourage broader industry investment in this critical challenge. If you're interested in helping make our models and products more robust to prompt injection, consider applying to join our team.
